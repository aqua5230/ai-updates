繁體中文

報告畫面更俐落，超長數字不再被切斷

查看 HTML 報告時，介面變得更清爽易讀了。以前報告裡會把「你的訂閱」跟「工具用量」拆成兩塊講同一件事，現在它們合併成「你的工具」卡片，方案名稱和花費等資訊統整在一起，不用再上下對照。另外，頂部的關鍵指標（KPI）卡片也調整了寬度比例，把最佔空間的 TOKENS 欄位拉寬，確保就算你用了幾十億的 token（AI 處理文字的單位），數字也能完整顯示，不會因為視窗太窄被卡掉一半，而且數字切換時會對齊得像收銀機的螢幕一樣整齊。就像去銀行刷存摺，以前帳戶資訊跟交易明細印在不同頁要翻來翻去，而且金額太大印表機還會印出格子外，現在全部集中在一頁，數字再大也印得清清楚楚。

說明文件大翻新，新手上路不再迷路

第一次安裝這個工具的說明文件（README）變得更友善了。原本落落長的文件經過大幅瘦身，隱私保護承諾、系統需求跟快速上手指南全被移到了最顯眼的開頭，而且三種安裝方式也排在同等重要的位置任你選，不再有主從之分。一些囉唆的功能條列和標點符號被刪減，給開發者看的生硬指南也被移進了專屬的 docs/DEVELOPMENT 資料夾裡，不會再干擾一般使用者。就像遊樂園的導覽手冊，以前把逃生路線跟維修人員手冊全混在一起印，遊客找個廁所都要翻半天；現在第一頁就是遊園地圖跟買票指南，維修手冊則另外印給員工看。

English

Report layout gets cleaner and massive numbers stop getting cut off

The HTML report interface is now much cleaner and easier to read. Previously, the report described the same tools across two separate "Your subscription" and "By tool" sections; they are now merged into a single "Your tools" card where your plan details and cost stats sit together, sparing you the back-and-forth scrolling. Furthermore, the top KPI cards have been rebalanced to give the TOKENS column the widest slot. This ensures that even if you've consumed billions of tokens (the units AI uses to process text), the massive number won't be truncated or pushed out of bounds on narrow windows, and the digits line up as neatly as a cash register display. It's like updating your bank passbook: previously, your account info and transaction history were on different pages, and massive balances would print off the edge of the paper; now it's all cleanly consolidated on one page, and every digit fits perfectly.

README gets a major overhaul to keep new users from getting lost

The setup documentation (README) has been made much friendlier for first-time users. The formerly massive document has been significantly trimmed down: privacy promises, system requirements, and the quick start guide are now front and center at the very top. The three different installation methods are now presented on equal footing for you to choose from, rather than burying one under the others. Wordy feature bullets and punctuation have been tightened, and the highly technical developer guide has been safely relocated to its own docs/DEVELOPMENT folder so it no longer clutters the experience for regular users. It's like a theme park brochure: they used to print the emergency exits and the mechanic's maintenance manual all mixed in with the attraction list, making tourists flip endlessly just to find a restroom; now the first page is purely the park map and ticketing guide, while the mechanic's manual is printed separately for the staff.
