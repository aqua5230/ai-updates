Usage 0.34.25

Released: 2026-11-21

Release notes

BUGFIX

Button and Usage Unit Display Fixes Across All Panels

Looking at the numbers and buttons in a panel used to leave you unsure how many days were counted or what the unit was. Every panel's project range button now reads Today ⇄, matching the Antigravity Gemini ⇄ button, so you can tell at a glance that clicking it steps through Today, Yesterday, 7 Days, Month, and All Time. After v0.5.0 switched the panels to a web layout, the unit next to project and daily totals went missing. Every full count is now followed by a smaller, quieter tokens (text units AI uses to measure usage), such as 567,641 tokens, while the numbers stay exact and un-abbreviated. The narrowest panels shrink the project name slightly so a 13-character name still fits beside a nine-digit count. Previously, hiding Claude Code moved the Settings button to the next card right up against the title, or right next to the Gemini button on the Antigravity card. It now stays at the far right edge, just as it does on the Claude Code card.

BUGFIX

Windows 95 Panel Text Sizes and Bar Adjustments

In the Windows 95 panel, card titles used to be smaller than the rows below them, and the Gemini ⇄ button was a size smaller than the other title-bar buttons. Six text sizes are now consolidated into three: card titles, row labels, percentages, and the daily total are 15 px (pixels, the dot units on a screen). Project rows, the rate line, and all buttons are 13 px, while reset times, costs, and the credit line are 11 px. Project bars also grew from 3 px to 8 px tall, and 9 px for the top project. Previously, subtracting the beveled border from 3 px left only about 1 px of green, making it impossible to compare lengths, whereas now you can tell them apart at a glance.

Picture a handwritten ledger with erratic font sizes and hairline progress bars being redrawn with neat large, medium, and small rulings and thick bars, so the biggest expense stands out immediately.
FEATURE

Unified Style and Spacing for Four Panels Including Newspaper

Several themed panels used to have parts that clashed with their surroundings or felt cramped vertically, and they are now aligned with the rest of each card. In the Newspaper panel, project bars used to be thin rounded strips and now use the same square, outlined track as the quota bars. The Gemini ⇄ button switched from pill-shaped to square like the Settings button, and the Antigravity icon now sits on a gray square like the Claude Code icon. The Prism Arcade panel's project usage card used to show just the letter P, looking like an unfinished placeholder. It now displays a folder with small bars inside, placed in the same badge style as the Antigravity and Grok icons. The bottom card in the Cloud Observation, Aquarium, and Prism Arcade panels also has more breathing room. The rate line and daily total used to sit just 5 px from the center divider, and the gap is now 8 px on both sides, matching the space above the buttons.

BUGFIX

Panel Text and Buttons No Longer Covered or Blending In

Reading data gets hard when background colors fade out or decorative lines cross over card content. In the Cloud Observation panel, the Gemini ⇄ button used to be such a pale purple that it nearly vanished on the white glass card, and it now uses the same deep purple as the Antigravity icon. The icon's backing circle also changed from translucent white to light purple so it no longer blends into the card. In the Heart Monitor panel, the quota bars used to sit only 2 px below the reset time line, with the 80% tick poking into the text; the gap is now 6 px. The Origami panel's first-place badge used to be half light and half dark, causing the white 1 to disappear, and is now two shades of deep blue. The folded corner at the bottom right of each card is also smaller and dark only at the tip, keeping reset times, percentages, and costs on light paper. In the Lepidoptera panel, butterflies flying across the middle of the cards used to reach 85% opacity, covering reset times, the Settings button, and the status line with their outlines. Their peak opacity is now down to about 45%, so they still fly across the cards while the text underneath stays clear.

FEATURE

Catppuccin Restores Letter Case and Labels Each Flavor

The Catppuccin panel used to force project names like esun-daytrade into all-caps ESUN-DAYTRADE, and even the daily total showed TOKENS while project rows read tokens. Project names and the unit now keep their original case, while labels and buttons stay uppercase. The four squares at the bottom for switching flavors used to have no text, making the three dark ones nearly indistinguishable side by side. Each square now has its name—Latte, Frappé, Macchiato, and Mocha—labeled underneath, with the selected one highlighted.

Like putting original-case name tags back onto unlabeled, all-caps color swatches in a sample box, both projects and flavors are now recognizable at a glance.
FEATURE

Maple Dusk Fallen Leaves and Black Hole Starfield Details Updated

In the Maple Dusk panel's two bottom corners, three fallen leaves used to crowd into about 50 px on each side, some sideways or upside down and overlapping the card border. Each corner now has two leaves—one large and one small—lying nearly flat with a soft shadow below the bottom card. In the Black Hole panel, the 120 background stars no longer look like a static sticker. Previously only 8 stars brightened slightly, leaving the backdrop motionless while the black hole spun. Now 40 stars fade in and out at their own pace, and the six largest flash a tiny cross-shaped glint at their brightest.

Think of rearranging crooked paper cutouts crammed in the corners and swapping dim background bulbs for star lights that each twinkle on their own.
FEATURE

"Pick Up Where You Left Off" Hands Over the Full Session Ending

When you opened a new conversation to continue your work, the handoff note used to list only the commits (saved version records) Claude made, missing every merge, tag, and push you ran with !. Even when the last session was already finished, it still told Claude to propose a next step. Now it includes Claude Code's end-of-session recap when nothing happened after it, or the opening sentences of Claude's last reply otherwise. It also lists successful merged PRs (pull requests), created tags, and pushes from newest to oldest, and tells Claude to say a finished round is finished instead of inventing a next step. Compaction system text is no longer treated as your latest request, and messages queued while Claude was busy are now included. A request resent with extra detail is listed only once, and after /clear it prioritizes the session this window just cleared. The "uncommitted changes" line now says it describes the current working directory, because it is checked when the new conversation starts.

Just as a shift handoff used to record only what one person touched, the logbook now captures the other person's progress and how things wrapped up, closing out cleanly when the job is done.
