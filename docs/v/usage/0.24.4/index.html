大舉擴編十一組職業，並替高風險助手加上防護網

AI 人才市場迎來了一次大擴編。系統從上游（提供套件的源頭）同步了 11 個全新的職業組合包，總共增加 33 位新角色，包含客服、虛擬助理、電商營運、職涯教練、數據分析顧問、業務、遊戲工作室、人資招募、專案管理、廣告投放，以及產品顧問。同時，為了保護你的安全，這次也特別在合約審閱、報稅準備、設計報價這三個有法律風險的角色上，加上了避免「無牌照執業」與「智慧財產權歸屬」糾紛的提示詞防護網。這是一次純內容更新，沒有動到系統的程式碼。就像你的派遣公司今天一口氣開了 11 個新部門，而且為了怕惹上官司，還特別針對法務、會計和設計部門的員工發了免責聲明小卡，但公司本身的運作規則完全沒變。

English

Talent Market adds 11 role packs and bolsters safety guardrails on high-risk assistants

The AI Talent Market just received a massive expansion. By syncing from upstream (the original source of these packages), the system has added 11 brand-new role packs containing 33 new assistants, spanning fields like customer support, virtual assistance, ecommerce operations, career coaching, data analysis, sales, game development, HR recruiting, project management, paid media, and product consulting. Furthermore, to keep you safe, the contract-review, tax-filing-prep, and design-proposal-quote roles now feature built-in guardrails specifically addressing unauthorized-practice and intellectual property assignment risks. This is entirely a content update with no underlying code changes. It's like your temp agency simultaneously opening 11 new departments, while making sure the temps in the legal, accounting, and design teams are issued strict liability-waiver cards so you don't get sued—the agency itself still runs exactly the same way.
