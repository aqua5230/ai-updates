Usage 0.35.0

Released: 2026-11-22

Release notes

FEATURE

New "Plush Pals" Panel Theme

To make checking usage less dry, animals now keep you company while you watch your quotas. Go to **⚙ Settings → Switch Panel** and pick "Plush Pals", one of 18 themes, fixed in its light look regardless of system appearance. Cream cards with brown stitched edges sit above 11 original plush characters strolling along; click one to see its name, and now and then they chat or doze off. One character stands on each quota bar at its current percentage. Between the 5-hour and weekly quotas, the one closer to its limit looks worried at 80%, falls asleep when it runs out, and wakes up cheering when it resets. The pals only show speech bubbles when it matters, one at a time: when a quota hits 80%, runs out, or resets, or when a sleeping pal stretches about 10 minutes before reset. If the current pace will empty a quota early, it reports the time left and recommends a tool that still has room. Bubbles also appear when Claude or Codex reports an outage or recovers. When today's usage passes yesterday's, the whole row cheers, and there is a bubble after midnight too. On each day's first visit, the greeting adapts to a freshly reset weekly quota, returning after a few days, Monday or Friday, or the time of day. In December, snow falls, and a different felt Christmas tree stands at the end of the line each day.

Picture staring only at numbers before, whereas now plushies doze off at empty and cheer when refilled.
FEATURE

Beginner Mode Picks Better Terms and Marks Them One by One

When learning terms in Beginner Mode, you previously had to press 9 to mark all three even if you only knew one or two, and unrelated file names or paths often took up the three hint slots. Now pressing 4–6 marks just the corresponding term understood, while 9 still marks all three. Branch names, file names, paths, bare numbers, and PR (pull request) numbers no longer take up slots, and every hint actually appears in Claude's reply. Two names for the same concept now count as one term. Once you mark "分支" or "branch" understood, neither appears as a new hint; once you mark "PR" or "Pull Request" understood, "PR" and "pull request" stop popping up too. Typing either form of those terms yourself counts as knowing it.

Think of studying terms before like putting away a whole keyring with house numbers mixed in; now you can unclip and store each key on its own, and recognizing either the Chinese or English copy of the same key counts.
FEATURE

Beginner Mode Saves Progress and Matches Hints More Reliably

Small Beginner Mode hiccups like competing window saves and cut-off explanations are now fixed. When two conversations save at once, each change re-reads the term list right before saving so "understood" marks are less likely to be lost. When the list is unreadable, it no longer asks Haiku for terms first, and the 5-hour quota pause now follows that quota's own reading and reset times. Plain-language explanations now ask Haiku for a complete short sentence, and the length limit doubles to 80 characters so they are no longer cut in half. Any explanation saved half-finished is replaced with the full version the next time that term appears. A term learned under one spelling never comes back under the other, whichever you saw first. Replies containing only RAPID no longer count as showing API, and PR #42, Windows paths, and fix/test branch names no longer take up slots. When Haiku adds a line of preamble before the list, a single term with a missing field no longer causes the other terms to disappear, and each reply reads the term list fewer times. Now when multiple windows run in parallel, marks are less likely to drop, explanations show full sentences, and hints keep only the terms the reply actually uses.
