Usage 0.34.11

Released: 2026-11-04

Release notes

FEATURE

Report charts directly state conclusions, switching to intuitive bars and weekly trend charts

When you open the usage report to quickly grasp your overall call activity, you no longer need to perform mental calculations item by item over complex charts.

Picture receiving a reorganized health checkup summary: where full pages were once packed with dense lab numbers requiring line-by-line checks against reference standards, key conclusions are now printed right at the top of each section, with individual metrics condensed into clear progress bars.

Section titles across each area of the report now state their conclusion directly, such as "Claude Code leads with 66% of tokens", with a subtitle and data source line beneath the chart. Here, token (the text units AI processes and bills) composition has been changed into a single stacked bar. Tools, projects, and cache hit rates have all been switched to thin bars on a 0 to 100% scale. The weekly list is also turned into a column chart, marking the busiest and latest complete weeks for you.

FEATURE

Usage habit analysis is kept for an hour, opening reports without jank on heavy history

For users who have accumulated heavy historical records, opening the usage report in the past often required spending time waiting for reloads.

Just like a regular customer walking into a familiar cafe, the counter brings up your frequent preferences directly without flipping through past orders each time; meanwhile, daily sales and real-time prices on the cash register stay synchronized and refreshed at all times.

Analysis results in the usage habits section are now retained for an hour instead of being recalculated every five minutes as in the past. This allows most report opens to directly skip the process of rescanning all session (a single conversation history) files. All usage numbers and charts continue to update in real time without being delayed by retaining the analysis results.

BUGFIX

Fix quota recovery countdowns, reset displays, and Claude window scheduling

Readers who rely on usage tools to track model quotas can now regain real-time status when quotas are exhausted or reset. The menu bar has restored displaying the Antigravity quota recovery countdown. When quota is used up, it shows the remaining time until recovery and maintains a short update interval. Since 0.34.10, Claude Code stopped reporting the 5-hour window once it expired, causing auto-open to fail to detect the expiration and stall. The tool now remembers the last reset time it saw and opens the next window as usual once that time passes; plans without a 5-hour quota are unaffected. A display issue that also appeared since 0.34.10 caused the 5-hour and weekly rows to show -- after a reset, while quota recovery notifications were delayed until the next reply was sent. Now the hook (a small program automatically triggered by specific events) retains expired windows for one full cycle, allowing readers to display 0% and reset status as usual, and immediately sends recovery notifications upon reset. Plans without a 5-hour quota still remove the window after more than a full cycle, while weekly quotas continue to update.
