Usage 0.33.3

發布日期：2026-10-09

繁體中文

新功能

選單列常駐更輕巧，減輕處理器與記憶體負擔

長時間開著電腦工作，常駐在選單列的工具往往在不知不覺中吃掉大把電力與運算效能。

如同請了一位細心管家，以前客廳沒人也每分鐘全屋清點三次，離開房間還開著每盞燈；現在物品變動才核對，走出房門隨手關燈。

以前面板關閉時，每次刷新要花約 1.2 秒處理器時間，一分鐘可跑三次，工作時平均吃掉約 6% 運算效能。現在增量掃描不再因個別檔案變動就重列所有 Muse 工作階段，工作階段有變才重新解析，並把查好的 Claude 專案名稱放進快取（把算過或查過的結果暫存起來直接用），穩定狀態下刷新只要約 0.13 秒。關閉面板後，以前切換過的面板會各自殘留 WebKit 程序，最多達六個且佔用約 400 MB 記憶體；現在關閉後只保留目前這款，開著時切換的體驗維持不變。

新功能

Claude Desktop 額度穩定讀取，過期或被鎖檔不再缺漏

現在追蹤 Claude Desktop 用量，不必再擔心額度數字莫名變空白或紀錄突然中斷。

即使方案歷史紀錄已過期，只要最新歷史組織仍有新鮮的本機 HTTP 額度回應，就能繼續顯示百分比與重置時間；至於過期回應、無效組織以及其他帳號的快取（把查過的結果暫存起來直接用），則依然不會提供資料。當本機快取資料量超出第一個項目檔時，新的額度回應可能會被寫入 Chromium 的接續檔案，以前會因此漏掉，現在也能順利找到並讀取。遇到 Claude 紀錄檔短暫被鎖住時，例如 Windows 防毒軟體正在背景掃描，以前用量會一直缺漏直到檔案變更或重開軟體，現在會在下次刷新時自動重試讀取。以前你會卡在防毒軟體一掃描或快取一分卷就看不到額度、非得重開程式才能恢復，現在這些邊緣狀況都能自動銜接，讓你隨時看得到剩餘額度。

新功能

模型計費回歸實際執行，報表名稱與定價不再漏失

如果在 AI 費用明細裡看到一長串難懂的原始代碼，甚至換了模型卻被整筆高價計費，肯定讓人一頭霧水。

猶如請不同專長師傅接力修繕，以前只要後半段換了資深師傅，前半段學徒做的一律全照資深工資收費；現在每一段工時都按實際動手的師傅計價，帳單上也直接印出清楚簡稱。

當你在 Codex 對話中途切換模型時，用量現在會依照實際執行的模型分別計價；以前較早的回合會全部照該工作階段（session，一段持續對話的互動歷程）最後使用的模型來算，這次更新後會重新解析一次 Codex 歷史紀錄以修正帳目。Claude Haiku 5.5 也有了專屬定價與簡稱，連不上線上價目表時費用不會再消失，報表也會直接顯示簡稱而非原始模型 ID。而自動啟動 5 小時計時的提示與對話框，針對 Antigravity 也已正確改標為 Gemini 3.8 Flash Low。

新功能

面板右鍵選單移除重新載入，不再讓面板變空白

許多人習慣在小工具畫面上按右鍵重新整理，以前這個動作卻可能讓面板變成一片空白。

就像遙控器上留了一顆容易誤觸的工程按鍵，以前隨手碰到會讓電視畫面變成一片空白，現在直接拆掉那顆按鈕，怎麼按都不怕出問題。

先前 v0.22.5 雖然曾嘗試修正這項問題，卻因為查錯了選單項目的屬性，導致你在面板上點擊滑鼠右鍵選擇『重新載入』時，依然會讓整個面板變成空白畫面。右鍵選單裡的『重新載入』已移除，這個問題已經修好。

English

FEATURE

Menu bar footprint is lighter, easing CPU and memory load

When working on a computer for long hours, tools residing in the menu bar often drain significant power and processing capacity unnoticed.

Think of hiring a meticulous housekeeper: previously, even when the living room was empty, they did a full inventory three times a minute and left every light on after leaving a room; now, they only check inventory when items change and turn off lights upon leaving.

Previously, when the popover was closed, each refresh consumed about 1.2 seconds of CPU time and could run three times a minute, taking up an average of about 6% of compute capacity during work. Now, incremental scanning no longer re-lists all Muse sessions whenever an individual file changes; it only re-parses when sessions change and caches retrieved Claude project names, reducing steady-state refresh time to about 0.13 seconds. After closing the popover, previously switched popovers each left behind WebKit processes, reaching up to six processes and consuming about 400 MB of memory; now only the current one is retained after closing, while the switching experience remains unchanged when open.

FEATURE

Claude Desktop quota reads reliably, no longer dropping when expired or locked

When tracking Claude Desktop usage, you no longer have to worry about quota numbers mysteriously turning blank or records suddenly dropping out.

Even if plan history is expired, percentage and reset time continue to display as long as the latest historical organization still has fresh local HTTP quota responses; expired responses, invalid organizations, and caches from other accounts will still not provide data. When local cache data exceeds the first entry file, new quota responses may be written to Chromium continuation files; previously missed, they are now located and read smoothly. When Claude log files are temporarily locked, such as during background scans by Windows antivirus software, usage previously stayed missing until files changed or the app was restarted; it now automatically retries reading on the next refresh. Where you previously lost sight of your quota whenever antivirus scanned or the cache split into multiple volumes—forcing an app restart to recover—these edge cases are now handled seamlessly so your remaining quota stays visible.

FEATURE

Model billing reflects actual execution, keeping report names and pricing intact

Seeing cryptic raw IDs on an AI expense breakdown—or being billed entirely at higher rates after switching models—is bound to cause confusion.

Picture specialized craftspeople taking turns on a renovation: previously, switching to a senior specialist midway meant earlier work by an apprentice was billed entirely at senior rates; now, each segment of labor is priced by who actually performed the work, and invoices clearly print clean short names.

When you switch models mid-conversation in Codex, usage is now priced separately according to the model that actually ran. Earlier turns were previously billed entirely under the session's final model, but this update re-parses Codex history to correct the records. Claude Haiku 5.5 now has dedicated pricing and a short name; costs no longer disappear when offline from online pricing tables, and reports display its short name instead of the raw model ID. Additionally, prompts and dialogs that auto-start a 5-hour timer are now correctly relabeled as Gemini 3.8 Flash Low for Antigravity.

FEATURE

Reload removed from popover context menu, preventing blank screens

Many users are used to right-clicking on widget screens to refresh, but this action previously risked turning the popover completely blank.

Consider an easily bumped engineering button left on a remote control: previously, an accidental touch turned the TV screen blank, but removing that button entirely ensures nothing goes wrong no matter what you press.

Although v0.22.5 previously attempted to address this issue, checking the wrong menu item property meant that right-clicking on the popover and selecting 'Reload' still caused the entire popover to become a blank screen. 'Reload' has now been removed from the context menu, fixing this issue.
