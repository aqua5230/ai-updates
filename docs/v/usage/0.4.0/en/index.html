Usage 0.4.0

Released: 2026-05-20

Release notes

FEATURE

Panel system completely revamped with multiple cool themes to choose from

You can now freely switch between various usage panel styles to suit your mood.

The default panel, which previously only had one look, has been rebuilt from the ground up using modern web technologies (HTML/CSS). This not only adds subtle press-shrink feedback to buttons and reduces the white flash on opening, but also introduces several new choices: a dark Minimal style, a medical monitor waveform (ECG, where the waveform amplitude scales with usage percentage and higher burn rates produce more intense rhythms), a digital-rain Matrix style, and a localized Taiwan panel featuring its flag. It's like upgrading a basic digital watch that only tells time into a smartwatch where you can download cool animated faces anytime, and it runs smoother than ever.

FEATURE

Antigravity support added to keep a tight grip on your third AI wallet

You can now keep track of your Antigravity usage simultaneously, so you don't have to worry about overspending on a third AI tool.

The panel now features a dedicated card for it, clearly splitting your current usage (Session) and weekly cap (Weekly). Even smarter, if you haven't touched your quota at all, it politely hides the reset time instead of endlessly displaying a meaningless "reset in ~24h". It's like your financial manager not only keeping an eye on your original two wallets but now taking care of a third new one as well, and never bothering you when there's no money moving inside it.

FEATURE

Progress bar colors shift with usage so you can spot an empty tank instantly

You can now gauge how tight your usage is at a mere glance, without pausing to read the exact numbers.

The progress bars for each tool are no longer stuck on a single color; they now dynamically shift warning colors based on how much quota you've burned: below 50% stays the original brand color (Claude's orange or Codex's cyan), over half turns an amber yellow to remind you, and reaching 80% or above flashes straight to a warning red. It's just like a car's gas gauge—green normally, yellow when running low, and blaring red when it's hitting empty—allowing you to catch the danger in your peripheral vision instantly.

BUGFIX

Refreshing no longer freezes, with massive under-the-hood bug fixes and performance boosts

Your screen will no longer suffer annoying momentary freezes when you click the menu bar.

Previously, every usage refresh would cause the interface to stutter slightly; these read operations have now been shoved to the background to ensure it opens instantly. In addition, the system has fixed a mountain of minor bugs that might have startled you: cost estimates no longer get permanently stuck on stale numbers when your network is flaky, the interface stops throwing random errors, and the logic for grabbing usage data (including filtering out old logs and isolating corrupted record files) is now far more precise. The pricing cache (saving fetched prices locally to avoid looking them up again) even expires automatically after seven days so you aren't staring at outdated, expensive numbers after a model price drop. It's like asking your butler to stop sorting the bills in front of you in the living room, and instead quietly balance the books in the back office before handing you the final tally — never interrupting your actual work.

FEATURE

Renamed and bundled: double-click to launch with zero terminal commands and automatic cleanup

You no longer have to crack open a terminal and type a bunch of commands just to install and run it.

The tool has been officially renamed to "usage" and neatly bundled into a standalone macOS application (.app) that you can simply double-click to launch. No matter which folder you drop it in, the built-in hook (a script that grabs usage in the background) automatically recognizes its surroundings and finds the correct paths to install itself. If you've used the older version before (back when it was called "usag"), it politely sweeps away specific v0.1.x leftover paths and startup items, while explicitly leaving your existing configuration paths and binary names untouched so it won't break your current setup. It's like taking a piece of furniture that previously required you to read a manual and screw it together yourself, and turning it into a fully assembled product straight out of the box — it even takes out specific previous-generation trash for you while preserving the layout you're used to, and comes with a much prettier name printed on it.
