三個 AI 開圓桌會議，你給題目、指定誰上場

要決定一件事，問一個 AI 只會得到一種說法。新的 AI Council 從選單列開一個視窗，讓 Claude Code、Codex 和 Antigravity 針對你出的題目輪流討論。你挑誰上場、各自用哪個模型（Claude 選 Opus、Sonnet 或 Haiku，Codex 選 Terra、Luna 或 Sol，Antigravity 選 Gemini 系列），再定 1 到 5 輪，還可以指派其中一位當主持人在最後寫總結。比方你在猶豫要不要把資料庫換掉，就把這題丟進去讓三邊互相質疑。討論一開始，設定面板會收合成一條狀態列，把整個視窗高度讓給逐字稿和總結。

開會前先看得到帳單，第三輪起會亮黃燈

讓三個 AI 吵五輪聽起來很過癮，帳單卻不是線性成長。開始前的預估現在除了會呼叫幾次，還會直接告訴你要花掉多少文字單位，也就是實際計費的量。而且只要你把輪數拉到 3 輪以上，數字就會轉成琥珀色，並用「大約是兩輪的幾倍」這種講法提醒你。原因不難懂：每多一輪，每個參與者都得把前面所有人講過的話重新讀一次，讀的東西越堆越厚。像影印一本會議記錄，每加開一場會，下次要影印的頁數就比上次更多，不是多印一張而已。

把截圖和專案資料夾一起帶進會議室

光靠文字描述一張跑版的畫面，你自己都講不清楚。現在題目欄可以直接拖進圖片，或按 Cmd+V 貼上，參加討論的每個 CLI 都讀得到那張圖。你也可以掛上一個工作目錄，讓它們翻得到專案裡的真實檔案再發言，而不是憑空猜你的程式長什麼樣。這個目錄是唯讀的，它們看得到、改不動。像把設計稿和一整份卷宗帶進會議室發給大家，資料攤開讓人討論，但正本不會被誰順手改掉。

en

AI Council: three CLIs debate your topic in rounds

Ask one AI and you get one opinion. The new AI Council opens a menu-bar window where Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity discuss a topic you set, in rounds. You choose which ones take part and what model each runs (Opus, Sonnet or Haiku for Claude; Terra, Luna or Sol for Codex; a choice of Gemini models for Antigravity), set anywhere from one to five rounds, and can nominate one participant to act as moderator and write the closing summary. Weighing up whether to switch databases? Put the question in and let the three of them push back on each other. Once a discussion starts, the setup panel collapses into a status bar so the transcript and summary get the full window height.

See the council's bill before you start it

Letting three AIs argue for five rounds sounds fun, but the bill does not grow in a straight line. The estimate shown before you start now reports a token total — what you are actually billed on — rather than only a count of CLI calls. Push the round count to three or more and the figure turns amber, with a plain comparison against what two rounds would cost. The reason is simple: every extra round has each participant re-read everything said so far, and the transcript only gets thicker. Photocopying the minutes of a meeting works the same way — each additional session means more pages next time, not one more sheet.

Bring screenshots and a read-only project folder into the room

Describing a broken layout in words defeats even the person looking at it. You can now drag images straight into the topic box or paste them with Cmd+V, and every participating CLI gets to read them. You can also attach a working directory so they can consult the real files in your project before speaking, instead of guessing at what your code looks like. That directory is read-only: they can see it, they cannot change it. It is like handing out the mockups and a full case file in a meeting room — the material is open for discussion, but nobody walks off having edited the original.
