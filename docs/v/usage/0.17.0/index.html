Usage 0.17.0

發布日期：2026-06-10

繁體中文

新功能

新增「蝶類圖鑑」面板，把你的用量變成工程藍圖

你現在可以把用量面板換成充滿科技感的「蝶類圖鑑」（Lepidoptera）主題了。

這款新面板致敬了 Fable 5 發表的藍曬工程圖風格——在深普魯士藍的底色上，畫滿了青色的工程方格與裁切標記，並把 Claude Code 與 Codex（兩套 AI 工具）的標誌嵌進青色的定位框裡，數字也全換成等寬的工程字體。最酷的是，畫面上會有多隻用白色技術線稿畫成的蝴蝶（帶有構造圓、中軸線和翼展尺寸標註），牠們會在面板上緩緩拍動翅膀並四處飄移（如果你會暈眩，開啟系統的減少動態設定就能讓牠們停下來）。就像把原本枯燥的報表，換成一張從科幻電影裡拿出來、還有全息蝴蝶在上面飛舞的精密設計圖。你可以從「更換面板」裡選用它。

English

FEATURE

New "Lepidoptera" panel turns your usage into an engineering blueprint

You can now switch your usage panel to the high-tech "Lepidoptera" theme.

This new panel pays homage to the cyanotype blueprint style of the Fable 5 launch — on a deep Prussian-blue background, it draws a cyan engineering grid with corner crop marks, mounts the Claude Code and Codex (two AI tools) logos into cyan registration frames, and uses monospace engineering fonts for all readouts. Best of all, white technical line-art butterflies (drawn as schematics with construction circles, centerlines, and wingspan dimensions) will drift and beat their wings across the panel (if motion makes you dizzy, turning on your system's reduced motion setting will keep them still). It's like replacing a boring spreadsheet with a precision schematic straight out of a sci-fi movie, complete with holographic butterflies flying over it. You can pick it from the "Switch Panel" menu.
