Antigravity 卡片不再誤報配額用盡，還你真實的剩餘用量

現在查看 Antigravity 卡片（usage 裡顯示 Antigravity 帳號 AI 配額用量的資訊卡）時，不會再被明明還有很多可用量、卻顯示「100% 已用」的假警報嚇到了。以前卡片在決定顯示多少用量時，會去挑兩個配額群組（quota groups，計算不同 AI 模型使用量的分類，分為 Gemini 或 Claude/GPT）中消耗最嚴重的那一個。因此，只要你的 Claude/GPT 五小時視窗（five-hour window，這類模型計算額度的時間重置區間）被耗盡，就算你帳號實際上在跑的 Gemini 群組還有滿滿的可用量，卡片還是會誤報成額度完全用光。現在這個顯示邏輯修好了，卡片會始終專注追蹤 Gemini 群組的狀態，只有當 API response（伺服器回傳給程式的資料）裡完全找不到 Gemini 的數據時，才會退而求其次去看消耗最多的群組。就像拿雙卡雙待的手機，以前只要你很少用的副卡網路流量用完，手機畫面就會一直跳出「流量耗盡」的警告，明明你真正上網用的主卡還有一大堆流量；現在螢幕終於會乖乖顯示主卡的真實流量，除非主卡不見了才會去看副卡。

en

Antigravity card stops falsely reporting exhausted quotas, showing your real remaining usage

When you check your Antigravity card (the info card in usage showing the AI quota of your Antigravity account), you'll no longer be jumpscared by a "100% used" fake alarm when you actually have plenty of usage left. Previously, to determine what to display, the card would simply pick the more depleted of the two quota groups (categories for tracking different AI model usage, specifically Gemini vs. Claude/GPT). Because of this, if your Claude/GPT five-hour window (the resetting time frame over which these models calculate their limits) was exhausted, the card would falsely report your entire quota as maxed out, even while the Gemini group that your account actually runs on still had plenty of capacity. This display logic is now fixed so the card always reliably tracks the Gemini group, and it will only fall back to showing the most-depleted group if Gemini is ever completely missing from the API response (the data the server sends back to the program). It's like using a dual-SIM phone: your screen used to constantly flash a "Data Exhausted" warning just because you ran out of data on your rarely-used secondary SIM, even though your primary SIM—the one you actually use for internet—had plenty of data left. Now the screen properly reflects your primary SIM's true data usage, and only looks at the secondary one if the primary SIM is removed.

原始 CHANGELOG

Original changelog
**Antigravity card no longer misreads as maxed out**: the card used to show whichever of the two quota groups (Gemini, or Claude/GPT) was most depleted, so an exhausted Claude/GPT five-hour window read as "100% used" even while the Gemini group the account actually runs on still had plenty left. The card now always tracks the Gemini group (falling back to the most-depleted group only if Gemini is ever absent from the API response).
