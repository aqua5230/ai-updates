繁體中文

報告可點 Share 分享，還能自動把專案名稱打碼

現在你可以把分析報告輕鬆傳給主管或同事看了。報告右上角多了一顆分享按鈕，點開就能選擇「另存一份 .html」或「複製檔案路徑」，透過 AirDrop、信箱或通訊軟體傳送，對方用手機或電腦的瀏覽器都能直接打開看。而且，下載介面裡多了一個預設打勾的「隱藏專案名稱」選項，勾著下載的話，檔案裡的專案名字都會被自動替換成「Project 1、Project 2」這類代號，不用擔心機密外洩，而你螢幕上看的畫面仍然維持原樣。就像你拿提款機的明細給別人看，以前要自己拿黑筆塗掉帳號，現在列印時機器就自動幫你打好星號了。

介面大掃除，終端機與報告畫面更俐落

這次為終端機的狀態列（statusLine，在輸入框上方顯示額度的區塊）和分析報告做了視覺瘦身。狀態列移除了原本顯示累計問答、快取和花費的第二行，讓版面更清爽，只把最重要的限額和模型資訊留在第一跟第三行。報告畫面的上方指標卡片（KPI 卡）也調整了寬度比例，把 token 與成本的卡片拉寬，避免累積到上億的長數字被折到下一行；報告底部則重新設計了贊助區塊，用會微微晃動的品牌標語取代了原本死板的頁尾文字，並補上 GitHub 連結。就像把儀表板上平時用不到的繁雜指針拆掉，並把最重要的油量表放大，讓你開車時一眼就能看清重點。

English

Share your report in one click with built-in project name redaction

You can now easily share your usage analysis with managers or colleagues. A new Share button in the top-right corner of the report lets you "Download .html" or "Copy file path" to send via AirDrop, email, or messaging apps, and recipients can open it in any browser on mobile or desktop. Plus, there's a privacy-first "Hide project names" checkbox in the download modal that's checked by default. When enabled, all project names in the downloaded file are automatically swapped to generic labels like "Project 1" or "Project 2" to prevent leaking sensitive information, while your on-screen view remains unchanged. It's like handing someone your bank receipt — you used to have to cross out the account number with a sharpie, but now the machine prints asterisks for you automatically.

Visual cleanup makes the terminal and reports much sleeker

We've put the terminal status line (the block above your input showing quotas) and the HTML report on a visual diet. The status line drops its second row that showed cumulative totals, cache, and costs, keeping the layout clean and focusing only on crucial limits and model info on the first and third lines. In the report, the top KPI cards have rebalanced widths — the tokens and cost cards are now wider so massive 9-digit numbers won't awkwardly wrap to the next line. We also reworked the sponsor section at the bottom, replacing the static footer text with a subtly wobbling brand slogan and adding a GitHub link. It's like removing the clutter of rarely-used gauges from your car's dashboard and enlarging the gas gauge so you can read what matters at a glance.
