Muse Code 1.4.16

發布日期：2026-11-13

繁體中文

新功能

編輯器可透過預設開啟的 muse acp 直接呼叫 Muse

平常在編輯器裡想直接叫出代理幫忙，還得先手動開啟介面往往多了一道工。現在 muse acp 改為預設開啟，像是 Zed 這類支援 Agent Client Protocol（讓編輯器與代理溝通的標準協定）的編輯器，都能直接把 Muse 當作代理來運作。當你打開 Zed 準備修改手邊的筆記或程式時，不用再另外開啟開關就能立即指派 Muse 執行任務，不過這組介面目前仍屬實驗性質，後續版本可能會再調整。

就像新買的設備出廠就已插好通用規格的轉接頭，以前得自己接線打開開關才能配對，現在只要編輯器支援同一套插座就能直接連上。
新功能

支援以 prompt.surfaces 與空白設定關閉開發者提示詞

對話剛起步時若想關掉一部分預設提示，過去較難照個別需要一一拿掉。在全域或專案設定裡加入 prompt.surfaces（可附帶選用的 default 預設值），或是透過工作階段設定覆寫，就能依 ID 關閉指定的開發者提示詞區塊。目前有五個受保護的區塊會繼續維持開啟，如果設定裡填了不認識的 ID，啟動時會跳過並顯示通知，而由工作階段直接送出未知 ID 則會遭到拒絕。當你想在特定專注寫作的工作階段完全退出 Muse 內建的開發者脈絡，只要在工作階段設定中把 developerPrompt 設為 null 或空字串即可。

跟點餐時勾選客製化選項一樣，以前固定配料會整盤端上桌，現在能按編號拿掉不要的區塊，或乾脆整份不加預設底料。
新功能

重開終端機工作階段可保存並套用新版開發者指令

中斷對話後想改掉背景規範再接著聊，以前重開工作階段往往還是沿用舊指令。現在每次工作階段啟動時都會先記錄開發者指令，只要你在設定裡修改 run.developer_prompt，再於終端機透過 --resume、--continue 或 tbh exec --session-id 重開工作階段，Muse 就會存下新版內容並從下一輪開始套用。對於由這一版建立或更新過開發者指令的工作階段，若某次執行正處於第一步，遠端的傳送、開啟、列表或輪詢操作可能會等到該步驟的檢查點完成，但絕不會超過呼叫本身的期限。其他既有工作階段則不受影響，不過現在新建立的工作階段無法用關閉此功能的舊版本開啟。

就像登機前先在櫃檯建檔規範，修改後重辦登機便會換上新版，起飛第一步完成前遠端查詢會稍等檢查紀錄，只是舊版閘門還讀不懂新檔案。
問題修復

工作階段語言鎖定改依使用者訊息於首段輸出宣告

專案夾雜大量英文程式碼或名詞時，你可能擔心對話回覆被裡頭的字詞帶偏語言。

好比飯店櫃檯直接聽住客本人說什麼語言就用那種語言回應，而不是看護照夾裡的英文備註或行李標籤亂猜，從第一句問候就說對語言，住客還沒開口前則先用英文招呼。

在符合條件的主執行流程中，Muse 現在會從第一段可見輸出就明確宣告你的工作階段語言釘選（鎖定這段對話該使用的回覆語言）。這個語言設定會固定依據你在該工作階段發送的訊息推算出來，不會再從名稱、程式碼或工具輸出推斷語言。當你輸入的內容還沒有明顯語言訊號時，則會採用英文作為預設語言。

問題修復

動手修改前核對更嚴謹並於受阻時先停下詢問

把專案交給代理查問題或修程式，最怕它太快下結論說找不到檔案，或自己繞過限制硬闖。

宛如老練的水電師傅進屋檢修，找不到管線先換幾個角落巡一遍、確認哪一戶報修、照原況重試一次再動扳手，碰到社區禁令或斷水閥門就停下來問屋主，絕不擅自繞道。

現在 Muse 動手前會把每一步核對得更仔細：宣告東西不存在前，會先試別種拼法與更寬的路徑；著手修復前，會先確認回報指向哪一份程式碼複本，並在原本出錯的環境設定下重跑具名的失敗測試。查案時也會讀取版本庫自己的歷史紀錄與上游分支。若變動受到版本庫規則禁止或管制，或是網路政策擋住某個步驟，它都會停下來詢問你，不再自己繞道處理。

問題修復

產出結果若落在別處直接補進指定位置

有時請代理產出內容，它卻把結果做在你最初指定以外的地方，還得你多點一次確認才肯搬過來。現在就算 Muse 把成果建在別的畫面、分頁、頁面或檔案裡，它也不會停下來問你要不要移過去，而是繼續完成並直接加進你當初要求的位置。當你在寫報告或整理筆記，指定要把段落補進某份檔案時，不用再多回一句「好，幫我放進去」就能直接看到結果到位。

猶如請人把整理好的資料放進指定抽屜，以前做完留在餐桌上還要問一聲要不要搬，現在會直接放進你說的抽屜才收工。
問題修復

長工作階段接近容量上限時改要簡短回覆

同一段對話聊久了、貼入的資料一多，最掃興的就是準備收尾時突然被一句超出容量擋在半路。

恰似裝行李快到登機箱極限時，工作人員不再直接拒收整箱物件，而是改請你挑最精簡的幾件塞進剩餘空間。

過去長工作階段只要輸入內容加上請求的輸出長度超過模型的脈絡視窗（模型單次能同時處理的文字容量），就會跳出錯誤並強制停下。現在當脈絡視窗快滿時，Muse 會主動請模型改給較簡短的回覆，讓工作階段繼續進行而不致中斷。

問題修復

含大量截圖的工作階段壓縮後最多保留約八張近期原圖

除錯或做設計時常在對話裡貼進一張張螢幕截圖，以前這類工作階段一旦執行壓縮，很容易直接跳過存檔，導致後面想接續對話無從接起。現在壓縮近期回合含大量完整尺寸圖片（例如截圖）的工作階段時，多數情況都能順利存下檢查點，讓你從該處繼續接續工作階段。壓縮後的脈絡會原封不動保留最近最多約八張完整尺寸的圖片；較早的圖片會改為摘要；若單批超過八張，該整批也會改為摘要。

如同整理旅行相簿，以前塞滿高畫質相片就整本卡住封存不了，現在可留下部分近期原片，把舊相片改寫成摘要；單批超過八張則整批摘要歸檔。
問題修復

工作階段壓縮後分支或重開保留目標與背景復原

工作階段經過壓縮後，無論是從中開出新分支還是重新打開接續，過去都容易在交接處出錯。

想像把長篇筆記精簡裝訂成精華冊，以前拆成新副本或重翻頁面時總會掉頁失聯，現在關鍵的索引與行程紀錄都穩穩留在冊子裡。

在 muse serve 中針對已壓縮的工作階段分支對話（包括從它啟動的子代理），即使後續回合的指令只留在壓縮檢查點裡，現在也不再因為被判成格式錯誤的工作階段日誌而失敗。 設有目標的工作階段重新開啟後再進行分支時，就算該工作階段曾經在某個回合進行到一半時執行壓縮，現在也能順利分支而不報錯。 把工作階段壓縮並重新開啟之後，原本在背後運行的背景代理復原能力現在也能完整保留下來。

新功能

精簡長工作階段大量子代理的存檔與記憶體佔用

接手跑了很久、中途派過不少子代理分頭做事的對話時，以前重開畫面常覺得越跑越沉，甚至等半天才看到回覆。

彷彿爬山途中把已經吃完的乾糧包裝收進回收袋輕裝上路，過去空盒子全都繼續背在肩上越走越沉，現在只留沿途還用得上的補給。

啟動大量子代理的長工作階段不會再突然停止儲存檢查點：只要某個子代理已經結束、主代理也已讀過它的結果（例如等待它完成），就算沒人手動關閉，只要對話不再提及它就會自動從存檔的工作階段中移除。 重開長工作階段時佔用的記憶體變少了，因為保留在記憶體裡的每筆工具任務紀錄體積都更小。 重開包含長串子代理歷史的工作階段後，畫面顯示回覆的延遲也獲得縮減。 重開帶有許多已完成子代理的工作階段時，首次讀取每個子代理會耗用更少記憶體並減少處理工時，因為已完成子代理的工具歷史不再被複製進追蹤子代理嘗試次數的紀錄簿裡。

問題修復

補齊工作流程取消拒絕原因與暫停重開狀態

操作工作流程遇到取消被拒、暫停或重開接續時，以前往往摸不清失敗原因或弄丟額度設定。 當 muse serve 拒絕取消某個工作流程時，錯誤訊息裡新增的 stopRefusal 欄位會直接寫出確切的拒絕原因，例如 stale_revision。 如果取消工作流程的動作遇上工作階段儲存錯誤，muse serve 現在會附帶原因正式拒絕，不再直接回傳內部錯誤。 重新接續同一個 Workflow 時，使用者設定的 token（AI 處理與計費的文字單位）上限會原封不動保留下來。 暫停工作流程時，還沒跑完的背景工作不會再被誤報成遺失結果。過去在取消遇錯時只見內部錯誤、暫停時被報遺失結果，現在每種狀況都有清楚交代與保留。

問題修復

修正忙碌時的訊息排隊、收回與中斷重送行為

代理正在忙著處理任務時，你送出的新指令、插話或停止訊號以前常卡在交接空檔裡。

拿客滿時段的咖啡廳吧台來說，點單慢一點確認也不會把點單紙退回客人手裡，真要抽單而吧台塞滿時會等一下再請你重試，喊停時服務生也會再喊一聲確保停下。

當代理忙碌時送出或排隊的訊息，若代理確認較慢，不會再跳出「steer failed」並退回輸入框，而是會維持在排隊狀態並順利送達一次。 用來中斷子代理的訊息，在替代的子代理啟動後，就會停止顯示為排隊中。 在 muse serve 裡，若某個卡住的工作階段指令佇列已滿，你想收回排隊中的訊息時，不會一直等到工作階段復原，改成在 30 秒後回傳可重試的忙碌錯誤。 按下停止（Stop）時，如果主機回報正在忙碌，Stop 會再次送出中斷訊號，讓執行中的回合真正停下來，不會繼續往下跑。

新功能

工作階段協定為用戶端補齊狀態、錯誤與回執欄位

開發自己的操作介面時，若想即時掌握背景執行進度或失敗原因，這套通訊規範（session protocol）現在提供更明確的訊號。 父工作階段在同伺服器開啟時，讀取子代理 session/read 會帶 modelTurnRunning，標明是否仍在跑模型回合。 TurnErrorKind 新增兩個不可重試類型：reasoningReplayRefused 為換模型後無法接續，contextOverflow 為超出脈絡視窗，既有比對會由預設分支接住。 開啟 Bash 標題時，殼層指令 toolCall 會帶安全顯示的短 bashTitle，免解析模型原文即可標記。 主體完成回執會以專屬歷史列送達，逾時、被插話轉向或被壓縮的工具主體都能保住結果與展開狀態。

新功能

TypeScript SDK 支援傳入模擬主機函式進行測試

撰寫整合 TypeScript SDK 的工具時，如果測試流程一定得啟動真實的 muse 程序，在本機驗證時會綁手綁腳。

彷彿飛行員在地面用模擬駕駛艙練習起降流程，不必每次驗證儀表都把真飛機開上跑道。

現在 MuseClient.spawn 與 spawnMspConnection 都接受 spawnFn 選項，讓你能直接對記憶體內的模擬主機跑完交握與工作階段。當你準備測試用戶端與工具之間的連線流程時，只要透過 spawnFn 接入假主機，就能在記憶體中直接跑完整套工作階段互動，不必真的啟動 muse 程序。

新功能

特定主機工作階段可依管理政策於啟動時調整沙盒與網路

如果用戶端需要讓工作階段在更寬鬆的環境下運行，這項權限現在能在連線之初提出申請。由 muse serve --per-session 或 muse attach --stdio 啟動的主機上，只要用戶端選擇啟用實驗性 API，工作階段就能在 session/start 時關閉沙盒（隔離執行的安全環境）或開啟網路，前提是不能超出管理政策允許的範圍。在團隊允許連網存取特定資源的情境下，用戶端便能在啟動階段直接完成這項調整。

試想進大樓實驗室申請開放外網與解除隔離區，就算手上拿著專案的實驗通行證，能開關的幅度依然受到管理規章限制。
問題修復

muse serve 支援符號連結工作區以載入自訂代理

不少人習慣把專案資料夾放在別顆硬碟，再用捷徑掛進工作目錄，以前在 muse serve 裡這條捷徑卻會直接把工作階段擋在門外。現在只要受信任的工作區路徑是指向資料夾的符號連結（symlink，指向另一個位置的捷徑檔），muse serve 啟動工作階段時就能正常載入該專案的自訂代理，不會再跳出「source root is a symlink」的失敗錯誤。

就像把門市入口設在轉角的連通道，過去警衛看到招牌後面是通道就拒收貨件，現在只要確認屬於信任範圍，照樣放行店內的專屬團隊進駐。
問題修復

工作階段使用外掛期間重裝不再因權限差異失敗

外掛正被某個工作階段使用中卻想重新安裝，過去常因為執行環境不同而直接碰壁。

恰似更換大樓電梯的保養零件，若只因早晚班技師設定的檢查權限略有不同，不會當場停用退件，而是先讓正在搭乘的這一趟安全運行，等乘客全數離開後下次保養再換新。

現在如果兩次安裝只是因為執行時的 umask（決定新檔案預設權限的環境遮罩）不同，例如一次從背景服務安裝、一次從終端機安裝，造成外掛頂層資料夾權限有差異，重裝時就不會再失敗。使用中的外掛複本會暫時保留不動，等那些工作階段都結束後，在下一次安裝時才會替換。

新功能

當機或啟動中斷後可正常重開並列出取消呼叫

萬一工作階段在啟動途中被中斷，或程式突然當機，重新接續時最怕既進不去也搞不清楚剛剛卡在哪。現在啟動期間遭到中斷的工作階段，重新開啟時會恢復正常進入，不再直接失敗。當機後重新接續工作階段時，畫面會顯示一則通知，列出當機當時被取消的呼叫；如果現場有印出異常結束通知，這份清單會併入其中，沒有異常結束通知時則會單獨以警告方式出現。

好比停電復電後重啟作業台，開機時不再因為上次沒啟動完就卡死，螢幕還會印出一張紙條列出斷電當下被迫取消的通話。
問題修復

回合存檔曾失敗時的日誌寫入降級提示不再遺失

執行回合的半途若曾發生存檔失敗，結尾原本該顯示的警告提示過去在幾種情況下會悄悄不見。現在回合結束時的「session log flush degraded」提示不會再遺失。無論是回合稍早存檔失敗後中途由檢查點把工作階段日誌寫出，或是存檔失敗後該回合接著送出影片、結算了一筆跨重啟等待的工具核准，這則警示都會在回合結束時照常留下。

跟帳房在每日結算對帳一樣，只要當天曾有過寫帳失敗，不管中途有無補登或後來又附了影像與隔夜簽呈，結帳時依舊會把警示便條留在頁尾。
問題修復

非 UTF-8 路徑下的工作階段索引不再卡死關機

把工作階段資料夾放在含有非 UTF-8 字元的路徑底下時，以前一關掉程式就容易陷入停不下來的僵局。

猶如車用導航遇到認不得的亂碼路名時，不斷在原地重新計算路線而遲遲無法熄火停車，現在碰到這種奇怪路徑不再無止境重算。

當 sessions 資料夾位於不是有效 UTF-8（一種常見的電腦文字編碼標準）的路徑底下時，工作階段索引過去會反覆不斷地重寫自己，連帶讓整個程式卡在關機步驟。這項錯誤已經修好，現在工作階段索引不會再無止境重寫並卡死關機流程。

問題修復

終端機程式狀態在背景回合完成後正確回到閒置

自家回合曾執行失敗後，就算後續的背景排程已經順利跑完，終端機的狀態燈號以前還是會卡在舊錯誤上。在支援顯示程式狀態（OSC 7501，終端機用來接收程式狀態訊號的控制規格）的終端機裡，現在當你原本的回合失敗、之後有排程或其他背景回合完成時，狀態會正常回到閒置，不會在每次背景回合結束後又把那個舊錯誤重秀一遍。

若把終端機狀態燈比作洗衣機面板上的提示燈，以前上一次脫水故障亮過紅燈後，之後每次背景自動烘衣結束都會再亮一次舊故障燈，現在背景程序完成便會乖乖切回待機狀態。
新功能

執行 /goal resume 可直接顯示答案不必重開對話

用目標功能整理專案重點、等著看指令的答案時，若還得重新打開原本的對話才能看到結果，手上的工作就多了一道操作。使用 /goal resume 後，現在能直接看到該指令的答案，不必重新開啟對話。

好比在服務櫃檯等答覆，以前得重新推門進接待室才能聽到回答，現在站在櫃檯就能直接聽見；不必為了得到這句答覆，再把剛才離開的房門打開一次。
新功能

訊息核准預覽完整保留比較運算子與底線

準備核准代理送出的訊息前，螢幕上的預覽畫面是你確認文字有沒有出錯的唯一依據。如果待送出的內容裡包含比較運算子（例如用來判斷大小的 < 或 > 符號）或是像 user_name 這樣帶有底線的字詞，現在的訊息核准預覽都會原樣保留這些符號與底線。

假想印刷廠送印前給你一張校對樣張，過去某些大於小於記號和底線常在樣張上漏印，現在每個符號都安安穩穩印在紙面上供你逐字確認。

English

FEATURE

Editors can invoke Muse directly through default-on muse acp

When you want an agent's help directly inside your editor, having to open an interface by hand first often adds an extra step. Now muse acp is turned on by default, so editors that support the Agent Client Protocol (a standard way for editors and agents to communicate), such as Zed, can run Muse directly as their agent. When you open Zed to edit your notes or code, you can assign tasks to Muse right away without flipping another switch, though this surface is still experimental and may change in a later release.

A newly bought device ships with a universal adapter already plugged in—previously you had to wire it up and flip a switch to pair, whereas now any editor with that same socket connects right away.
FEATURE

Turn off developer prompts with prompt.surfaces and blank settings

When starting a conversation, turning off only part of the default prompts used to be hard to tailor piece by piece. Add prompt.surfaces (with an optional default) to global or project settings, or override it in session settings, to turn off specific developer-prompt blocks by ID. Five protected blocks stay on for now; an unrecognized ID in settings is skipped with a startup notice, while sending an unknown ID directly from a session is refused. When you want a focused writing session to opt out of Muse's built-in developer context completely, set developerPrompt to null or an empty string in that session's config.

Like checking custom options when ordering a meal, fixed toppings used to arrive all at once on the plate, whereas now you can remove unwanted items by number or skip the default base altogether.
FEATURE

Reopened terminal sessions save and apply updated developer instructions

If you paused a conversation and wanted to change its background guidelines before continuing, reopening the session used to keep the old instructions. Muse now records developer instructions each time a session starts, so when you edit run.developer_prompt in your settings and reopen the session in the terminal with --resume, --continue, or tbh exec --session-id, it saves the new text and applies it from the next turn. On a session whose developer instructions were created or updated by this release, a remote send, open, list, or poll made while a run is in its first step may wait until that step's checkpoint finishes, never past the call's own deadline. Other existing sessions are unaffected, though sessions created now cannot be opened by an older build that has this feature turned off.

Think of filing guidelines at the check-in counter before boarding: updating them and checking in again switches to the new version, remote inquiries wait briefly for the checkpoint before the first step of takeoff finishes, and older gates cannot read the new file yet.
BUGFIX

Session language pin now declared from first output based on user messages

When a project mixes in lots of English code or terms, you might worry that replies will be swayed by those words into the wrong language.

Picture a hotel front desk listening directly to what the guest speaks and replying in kind rather than guessing from English notes in a passport holder or luggage tags, getting the greeting right from the first word and defaulting to English before the guest speaks.

On eligible main runs, Muse now explicitly states your session language pin (which locks the reply choice for this conversation) from the first visible output. This language setting is derived deterministically from the messages you send in the session, rather than inferred from names, code, or tool output. When your input carries no clear language signal yet, English is used as the default.

BUGFIX

Stricter checks before editing and pausing to ask when blocked

When handing a project to an agent to investigate or fix, it is frustrating if it jumps too quickly to saying a file is missing or forces its way around limits.

A seasoned plumber inspecting a house checks other corners before saying a pipe is missing, confirms which unit filed the report, and retries under the original conditions before touching a wrench, stopping to ask the homeowner rather than routing around a building rule or shutoff valve.

Muse now verifies each step more carefully before acting: it tries other spellings and wider paths before saying something does not exist, confirms which copy of the code a report points to before fixing it, and reruns named failing tests in the setup where they failed. It also reads the repository's own history and upstream branch while investigating. If a change is forbidden or gated by a repository rule, or if a network policy blocks a step, it stops to ask you instead of routing around it.

BUGFIX

Results built elsewhere are added directly to the requested spot

Sometimes you ask an agent to produce something, and it builds the result somewhere other than where you first asked, leaving you to confirm one more time before moving it over. Now even if Muse builds a result in a different screen, tab, page, or file, it will not stop to ask whether to move it, and instead finishes the work and adds it directly where you asked. When you are writing a report or organizing notes and ask for a section to be added to a specific file, you see it land in place without having to reply "yes, put it in."

Compare that to asking someone to put sorted papers into a specific drawer: before, they left them on the dining table and asked whether to move them, whereas now they place them straight into the drawer before wrapping up.
BUGFIX

Long sessions request shorter replies when nearing capacity

Nothing spoils the wrap-up of a long conversation packed with pasted material like suddenly hitting a capacity wall.

When packing a carry-on approaches the size limit, staff no longer turn away the whole bag and instead ask you to pick only the most compact items for the remaining space.

Previously, a long session stopped with an error whenever its input plus requested output exceeded the model's context window (how much text a model can handle at once). Now, when the context window is nearly full, Muse asks the model for a shorter reply so the session can keep going without stopping.

BUGFIX

Compacted screenshot-heavy sessions keep up to about eight recent originals

Debugging or designing often fills a conversation with screenshots, and compacting those sessions used to skip saving a checkpoint, leaving you with nowhere to resume. Now when compacting a session whose recent turns hold many full-size images (such as screenshots), it saves its checkpoint in most cases so you can resume from that point. The compacted context keeps up to about eight recent full-size images as they are; older images are summarized, and if a single batch has more than eight, that entire batch is summarized too.

Sorting a travel photo album used to stall the whole archive whenever it was packed with high-resolution shots, whereas now you can keep a portion of recent originals and turn older photos—or any single batch over eight—into summaries.
BUGFIX

Compacted sessions keep goals and background recovery when forked or reopened

After a session is compacted, both branching off a new conversation and reopening it to continue used to break at the handoff.

Imagine binding long notes into a condensed booklet: splitting off a new copy or flipping back through pages used to drop sheets and lose track, whereas now key indexes and trip records stay intact inside.

Forking a compacted session in muse serve (including any subagent started from it) no longer fails as a malformed session log even when later turns' instructions live only in the compaction checkpoint. Forking a resumed session that has a goal now succeeds without error even if the session was compacted mid-turn. Background agent recovery is now preserved after compacting and reopening a session.

FEATURE

Trim saved state and memory use for long sessions with many subagents

When picking up a long-running conversation that spawned many subagents to split the work, reopening the screen used to feel sluggish and take ages to show a reply.

On a mountain hike, packing empty ration wrappers into a recycling bag lightens the load—instead of carrying every empty box on your shoulders and growing heavier, you keep only the supplies still needed along the trail.

Long sessions that start many subagents no longer stop saving checkpoints: once a subagent finishes and the main agent has read its result (for example by waiting for it to finish), it automatically drops out of the saved session as soon as the conversation stops mentioning it, even if nobody closed it manually. Resuming a long session uses less memory because each tool-task record kept in memory is smaller. Reply display delays after resuming sessions with long subagent histories are also reduced. Resuming a session with many finished subagents uses less memory and less work when first reading each of them, because a finished subagent's tool history is no longer copied into the bookkeeping that tracks subagent attempts.

BUGFIX

Clarify Workflow Cancel Refusals and Preserve Pause and Resume State

When a workflow cancel was refused, paused, or resumed, it was previously hard to tell why it failed or your limit settings would be lost. When muse serve refuses to cancel a workflow, the new stopRefusal field in the error message now names the exact refusal reason, such as stale_revision. If canceling a workflow hits a session storage error, muse serve now formally refuses it with a reason instead of returning an internal error. When resuming the same Workflow, the user's limit on tokens (text units for AI processing and billing) is preserved intact. When pausing a workflow, unfinished background work is no longer misreported as a missing result. Previously, cancel errors only showed an internal error and pauses reported missing results, whereas now every case is clearly explained and preserved.

BUGFIX

Fix Message Queuing, Take-Back, and Interrupt Retries While Busy

When an agent was busy handling a task, new instructions, interruptions, or stop signals you sent often got stuck in handoff gaps.

Consider a packed coffee counter: even if an order takes a moment to confirm, the ticket is not handed back to the customer, a cancellation at a jammed counter waits briefly before asking you to retry, and a call to stop is echoed again so work actually halts.

A message sent or queued while the agent is busy no longer shows "steer failed" and returns to the input box if the agent is slow to confirm it; instead, it stays queued and is delivered once. Messages used to interrupt a subagent stop showing as queued once the replacement subagent starts. In muse serve, if a stuck session's command queue is full and you try to take back a queued message, it no longer waits until the session recovers and instead returns a retryable busy error after 30 seconds. When you press Stop, if the host says it is busy, Stop sends the interrupt signal again so the running turn actually stops instead of continuing.

FEATURE

Session Protocol Adds Status, Error, and Receipt Fields for Clients

When building your own interface, the session protocol now gives clearer signals for tracking background progress or failure causes in real time. When the parent session is open on the same server, reading a subagent via session/read now includes modelTurnRunning to indicate whether a model turn is still running. TurnErrorKind adds two non-retryable types: reasoningReplayRefused for chats that cannot continue after a model switch, and contextOverflow for exceeding the context window; existing matches are caught by their default branch. While Bash titles are enabled, a shell command toolCall carries a short, display-safe bashTitle so rows can be labeled without parsing the model's raw text. Body-completion receipts arrive as dedicated history rows, preserving the results and expansion state of timed-out, steered, or compacted tool bodies.

FEATURE

TypeScript SDK Supports Passing a Fake Host Function for Testing

When writing tools that integrate the TypeScript SDK, requiring a real muse process for every test makes local verification cumbersome.

Pilots practice takeoffs and landings in a ground cockpit simulator rather than rolling a real aircraft onto the runway every time an instrument needs checking.

Both MuseClient.spawn and spawnMspConnection now accept a spawnFn option, letting you run handshakes and sessions against an in-memory fake host. When you are ready to test the connection flow between a client and a tool, you can wire up a fake host through spawnFn and run the full session interaction in memory without starting a real muse process.

FEATURE

Sessions on Specific Hosts Can Adjust Sandbox and Network at Start Under Policy

If a client needs a session to run in a less restricted environment, that permission can now be requested right at connection time. On a host started by muse serve --per-session or muse attach --stdio, as long as the client opts into the experimental API, a session can turn off its sandbox (an isolated, secure execution environment) or open its network at session/start, provided it stays within what managed policy allows. In scenarios where a team permits network access to specific resources, the client can complete this adjustment directly during startup.

Entering a building lab to request external network access and lift isolation works the same way: even with an experimental project pass in hand, what you can toggle is still limited by management rules.
BUGFIX

muse serve Supports Symlink Workspaces to Load Custom Agents

Many people keep project folders on another drive and link them into their working directory with a shortcut, which previously caused muse serve to block the session at the door. Now, as long as a trusted workspace path is a symlink (a shortcut file pointing to another location) to a folder, muse serve starts the session and loads the project's custom agents normally instead of failing with "source root is a symlink".

Placing a shop entrance in a corner passageway used to make the guard reject deliveries upon seeing a corridor behind the sign, but once confirmed as trusted, the store's dedicated team is now let right in.
BUGFIX

Reinstalling a Plugin While in Use No Longer Fails on Permission Differences

Trying to reinstall a plugin while a session was still using it often hit a wall because the execution environment differed.

Replacing maintenance parts on a building elevator works the same way: if the only difference is slightly different inspection permissions set by morning and night technicians, the elevator is not halted on the spot, but allowed to finish its current trip safely and swapped out at the next service after all passengers exit.

Now, if two installs differ only in the permissions of the plugin's top folder due to different runtime umasks (environment masks that set default permissions for new files)—such as one from a background service and one from a terminal—reinstalling no longer fails. The copy in use is left alone for now and replaced at the next install after those sessions end.

FEATURE

Reopen Normally After Startup Interruption or Crash and List Cancelled Calls

If a session is interrupted during startup or the program suddenly crashes, the worst part of resuming is neither getting back in nor knowing where things stopped. Sessions interrupted during startup now reopen normally instead of failing. When resuming a session after a crash, a notice is shown listing the calls that the crash cancelled; if an abnormal-exit notice prints, this list is joined to it, and otherwise it appears on its own as a warning.

Restarting a workstation after a power outage works much the same: it no longer freezes at boot just because the last startup was cut short, and the screen prints a slip listing the calls forced to cancel when the power went out.
BUGFIX

Session Log Flush Degraded Note No Longer Goes Missing After Earlier Save Failures

If a save failed mid-turn, the warning note that was supposed to appear at the end previously vanished in several scenarios. Now, the "session log flush degraded" note at the end of a turn no longer goes missing. Whether a checkpoint flushed the session log mid-turn after an earlier save failure, or the turn sent a video or settled a tool approval that waited across a restart after a save failed, the warning still stays at the end of the turn.

Bookkeepers reconciling daily accounts do the same thing: as long as a ledger write failed earlier that day, regardless of mid-day catch-up entries or later videos and overnight sign-offs, the warning slip still remains at the bottom of the page at closing.
BUGFIX

Session Index Under Non-UTF-8 Paths No Longer Hangs Shutdown

When placing the sessions folder under a path with non-UTF-8 characters, shutting down the program previously got stuck in an endless loop.

Car navigation systems hitting an unreadable garbled street name behave similarly, endlessly recalculating the route in place and refusing to power down, whereas now such odd paths no longer trigger endless recalculation.

When the sessions folder is under a path that is not valid UTF-8 (a common computer text-encoding standard), the session index previously kept rewriting itself forever and hung the shutdown step. That bug is fixed, and the session index no longer rewrites itself endlessly or hangs shutdown.

BUGFIX

Terminal Program Status Returns to Idle After Background Turns Complete

After one of your own turns failed, a terminal's status indicator previously stayed stuck on the old error even when subsequent background schedules finished cleanly. In terminals that support showing program status (OSC 7501, a control specification for receiving status signals), the status now returns to idle when a scheduled or other background turn completes after your original turn failed, rather than displaying that old error again after every background turn.

A washing machine's panel light works the same way: after a spin-cycle fault turned it red once, every later automatic drying cycle in the background used to flash that same old fault light again, whereas now it switches back to standby once the background cycle finishes.
FEATURE

Running /goal resume Shows Answers Directly Without Reopening the Conversation

When you use the goals feature to organize project highlights and are waiting for a command's answer, having to reopen the original conversation just to view the result adds an extra step to your workflow. After running /goal resume, you can now see that command's answer directly without reopening the conversation.

Waiting at a service counter for a reply illustrates this well: previously you had to push open the door back into the reception room to hear the response, whereas now you can hear it right at the counter without opening the door you just walked out of again just to get that answer.
FEATURE

Message Approval Previews Preserve Comparison Operators and `_` Characters

Before approving a message for the agent to send, the preview on your screen is your only basis for checking whether any text went wrong. If the outgoing content contains comparison operators (such as < or > symbols for checking size) or terms with _ characters like user_name, message approval previews now preserve those symbols and _ characters intact.

Print-shop proof sheets work the same way: before a press run, you rely on a proof sheet where greater-than, less-than, and _ marks used to go missing, and now every mark stays printed on the page for you to verify character by character.
