Claude Code 2.1.195

發布日期：2026-06-26

繁體中文

新功能

語音輸入大進化：沒聲音會抓、中日泰文也能自動送出

用語音講話來輸入指令變得更聰明了。

以前對於不使用空格的語言（像是日文、中文、泰文），你講完話系統常常不知道你講完了、不會幫你自動送出；現在它終於學會聽懂這些語言的停頓並自動幫你送出了。在 macOS 上，如果你跑了很久的任務中間切換了麥克風設備，以前它會變成錄不到聲音的瞎子，現在修好這個毛病了；而在 Linux 系統上，它也能精準分辨出你到底是「沒有麥克風／錄音裝置」還是「缺少 SoX（一個音訊處理工具）」，不再丟出籠統的錯誤。這就像一個終於學會聽外語的打字員，以前你講中文他不知道你哪句是結尾，現在他能精準抓到你的句點並幫你交卷，而且麥克風插頭被踢掉他也會立刻發現。

新功能

滑鼠可半休眠、外掛與背景任務不再鬧脾氣

這次針對操作與背景工具修好了一些讓人煩躁的細節。

如果你不想在全螢幕時不小心點到畫面，現在你可以設定（CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_MOUSE_CLICKS）把滑鼠點擊和拖曳的功能關掉，但保留滾輪滑動的方便性。設定外掛（plugin）時，如果外掛的名稱帶有連字號（像 code-reviewer），以前系統會因為名字太像而抓錯人，現在會精準比對全名；就算外掛在商城的名字跟它內部寫的名字不一樣，你也能正常把它關掉或打開。另外，如果你用新版 Claude 啟動了背景任務，清單不會再因為看不懂就把它變不見；當掉的任務重新打開時也不會再讓你呆看 5 秒的全白畫面了。這就像微調你的辦公桌，滑鼠現在可以只准捲動不准點擊防誤觸，而且請來的外包人員（外掛）不會因為名字拼錯一個字就賴帳不工作，你的工作清單也不會因為軟體升級就神祕消失。

English

FEATURE

Voice dictation upgrades: catches silence, auto-submits CJK languages

Dictating commands with your voice is now much smarter.

Previously, for languages that are written without spaces (like Japanese, Chinese, or Thai), the system struggled to realize when you finished speaking and would fail to auto-submit your message; it now perfectly understands the pauses and submits them automatically. On macOS, if you swapped your microphone device in the middle of a long-running session, the app used to go deaf and just record pure silence; that's fixed. On Linux, it can now clearly distinguish whether you are entirely missing a microphone or just missing the required audio software, giving you exact error messages instead of vague ones. It's like a typist who finally learned a foreign language: they used to never know when your Chinese sentence ended, but now they punctuate and submit it perfectly, and they'll instantly notify you if your microphone cord gets unplugged.

BUGFIX

Mouse tweaks, plugin and background task fixes

Several frustrating details surrounding controls and background tools have been ironed out.

If you hate accidentally clicking things in fullscreen mode, you can now toggle a setting (CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_MOUSE_CLICKS) to completely disable clicks, drags, and hovers while still keeping the convenience of the scroll wheel. For plugins, if a tool's name contained a hyphen (like code-reviewer), the system used to lazily match parts of the name and load the wrong thing; it now strictly matches exact names. You can also successfully toggle a plugin on or off even if its internal code name differs from its storefront name. Furthermore, background jobs written by a newer version of Claude will no longer mysteriously vanish from your agent list, and if you reopen a crashed task, it instantly restarts instead of forcing you to stare at a blank white screen for 5 seconds. It's like customizing your workspace: your mouse can be locked to scrolling only so you never click by accident, your hired contractors (plugins) won't refuse to work just because of a typo in their name, and your to-do list won't magically erase itself just because you bought a newer pen.
